## 5. Proven code defects and fixes in this branch

1. **Sitemap `lastModified` was the generation time.** The production sitemap observed on 2026-11-22 returned the same `lastmod` (`2026-11-21T09:28:12.814Z`) for every static route and a `now` fallback for undated dynamic rows. `app/sitemap.ts` now delegates to `lib/sitemap/entries.ts`, which emits `lastModified` only for a parseable, real timestamp and omits it otherwise. Dates before 1971 (including the GSC 1970 placeholder) are treated as unknown.
2. **Dynamic filters were re-checked in code.** Flow rows now require `status=published` *and* `visibility=public`; issue letters require `status=published`; tags require a real slug instead of falling back to a display name; lots require `status=published`.
3. **Flow issue canonical/URL consistency.** `/flow-issues/<slug>` and `/en/flow-issues/<slug>` are two interface variants of one issue. Canonical, sitemap, archive links and previous/next links now use the issue content language (`lib/flow-issue/urls.ts`), not the archive chrome language. This removes the duplicate self-canonical pair without forcing a redirect.
4. **Redirecting / non-canonical static URLs remain excluded.** `/letters` (canonicalises to `/journal`) is excluded; `/stat`, `/projects` and `/feed` are redirects and never enter the sitemap.
5. **Journal 404s restored at the route level.** The full sitemap scan found six `/journal/*` URLs returning 404 while still listed in `sitemap.xml`. `app/journal/[slug]/page.tsx` now reads published rows with the service role (matching the root article route) and uses `limit(1)` instead of `.single()`, so request-scoped RLS or duplicate historical slugs cannot turn a published article into a 404. Two of those six are in the supplied 161-row list; the other four are outside it.
6. **`/flow/join` canonical host fixed.** The live page emitted `https://merkurov.love/flow/join` (non-www) because the canonical was relative to a production `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` without `www`. It now uses the shared `SITE_URL` constant and absolute canonical. Query-string `?lang=` hreflang values were removed because they are not separate URLs.

## 6. Not verified / cannot be concluded from this data

- The 33 *Crawled — currently not indexed* URLs could not be classified because their URLs were not supplied. Soft-404, thin-content, duplicate and canonical-mismatch conclusions for that group would require the drilldown.
- The 17 404, 5 noindex, 4 redirect, 2 alternative-canonical and 6 Google-selected-canonical URL lists were not supplied. No redirect, noindex or canonical change was made for them.
- The full sitemap scan found six `/journal/*` 404s. Two are in the supplied 161-row list; the other four are outside it. Their overlap with the unavailable 17-URL 404 group is a hypothesis, not a confirmed GSC mapping.
- A *Discovered — currently not indexed* state means Google knows the URL but has not crawled it yet. It is not proof of thin content, duplication or a canonical defect. No page was noindexed or deleted to reduce the count.
- Google indexing decisions and timing are outside the repository. No URL inspection or indexing API submission was performed.
- The live columns describe the currently deployed revision, not this branch. Re-run the script after an approved deploy to compare.
